# Laptop hardware fault and replacement

Use this runbook when the laptop battery swells, when the screen flickers, or when the keyboard fails on a corporate device.

The most common symptom is laptop battery swelling with the trackpad lifting. Users also report screen flicker on battery power and keyboard keys repeating on the laptop hardware.

Steps:

1. Confirm the asset tag and whether the laptop hardware fault is battery, screen, or keyboard.
2. For laptop battery swelling, tell the user to stop charging, power off, and bring the device to IT the same day.
3. For screen flicker, update the display driver and test on an external monitor to isolate the laptop hardware.
4. For keyboard keys repeating, clean the keyboard profile, test with an external keyboard, then order a top-case replacement.
5. Issue a loaner laptop and transfer the user data before the laptop hardware repair.
6. Confirm the repaired laptop passes diagnostics and the laptop hardware ticket is closed with the asset updated.

Common follow-ups: confirm the charger is the issued wattage, confirm screen flicker stops on AC power, and confirm the laptop hardware warranty covers the repair.

Escalate to hardware operations when laptop battery swelling is confirmed, when screen flicker affects a whole batch, or when keyboard replacement parts are out of stock.
